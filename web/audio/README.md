# సూత్ర స్వరము — recorded sutra audio

Pronunciation clips for the sutras of బాలవ్యాకరణము. Drop the files in this
folder; the app finds them automatically.

## Naming

    audio/<pariccheda>-<NNN>.mp3

`<NNN>` is the sutra number, zero-padded to three digits. `.m4a` is accepted
too — the app tries `.mp3` first, then `.m4a`.

## Notes

- A sutra with no clip shows no play button. The book works fine without it,
  so clips can be added gradually.
- A full checklist — one row per sutra with the exact filename — downloads from
  గ్రంథము → **ఆడియో జాబితా (CSV)**.
- Mono, 22–44 kHz is plenty; keep clips short and roughly level-matched.
- 81 of the 465 sutras carry the archaic candrabindu (ఁ) and 26 carry ఱ — those
  are the ones worth listening back to.
